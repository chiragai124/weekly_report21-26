Headline Takeaways

  • Community #4 carried almost all activity: 516 of 584 messages (88%). Its Germany group was the busiest single group with 205 messages.
  • Real peer guidance in #4: members explained Germany's 13-year education rule, Studienkolleg, the blocked account, living costs, Ausbildung and the Summer 2027 intake (portals close 15 Jan 2027).

  • Communities #1 and #2 were almost silent: 6 and 3 messages, with most country groups posting nothing.
  • IG Broadcast keeps growing: 792 members, up 341 since 6 Oct.
Needs Attention

  • Unsolicited posts: a room-for-rent listing in Hull posted twice (#1 UK) and a career-coaching promo (#3 UK).
  • Tone: a heated exchange between a few members in #4 Germany on 23 Oct; another member asked everyone to keep it respectful.
